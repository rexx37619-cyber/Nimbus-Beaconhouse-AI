About Nimbus 4.5 ROR

Nimbus 4.5 ROR is designed for guided learning instead of dumping broad copy-paste answers. For educational questions, Nimbus gives concise Keywords, a question-specific three-step Answer Structure, a short Example Answer starter, and an 8-word Key Fact prompt. This gives students enough direction to understand the topic, rephrase the idea in their own words, and strengthen critical thinking while still supporting Beaconhouse context, attachments, coding help, visuals, and file-ready outputs.
