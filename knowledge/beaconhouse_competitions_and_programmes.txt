NIMBUS BEACONHOUSE PUBLIC KNOWLEDGE — COMPETITIONS & PROGRAMMES

This file is a public-reference snapshot. It excludes student-winner names and private records.
Always check the linked official page for the newest schedule/rules.

1) BISC — Beaconhouse International Student Convention
Official: https://bisc.beaconhouse.net/
About: https://bisc.beaconhouse.net/about-bisc/
Public description: a global Beaconhouse student convention combining learning, collaboration and competition. Public pages describe sports, arts/creative, knowledge, gaming, science, entrepreneurship and culture-related activities. Public event listings include RoboQuest, Innovation Spark, BISC Icon, BISC Quiz, Art Extravaganza, Green Revolution Challenge, Film Festival, Quantum Quest and BISC Talks.

2) RISE — Beaconhouse online competitions platform
Official: https://rise.beaconhouse.net/
Public purpose: a Beaconhouse-hosted collaborative platform for national and international competition opportunities. Public listings have included Young Canvas, Beaconhouse Icon, BISC-related competitions and virtual sports competitions.

3) BEACONHOUSE ICON
Public RISE listing: https://rise.beaconhouse.net/
Public description: a national-level singing competition/platform for Beaconhouse Pakistan students.

4) YOUNG CANVAS
Public RISE listing: https://rise.beaconhouse.net/
Public description: an art-focused competition/event platform with public Young Canvas listings.

5) BISC SPORTS
Official BISC: https://bisc.beaconhouse.net/
Public event descriptions include athletics and other sports as part of the convention.

6) BISC CREATIVE / ARTS
Official BISC: https://bisc.beaconhouse.net/
Public categories include visual art, photography, digital art, mixed media and other creative activities.

7) STEAM COMPETITIONS
Official: https://www.beaconhouse.net/steam-competition/
Public purpose: Beaconhouse STEAM competition activities for science, technology, engineering, arts and mathematics-related student work.

8) SPORTS COMPETITIONS
Official: https://www.beaconhouse.net/sports-competition/
Public purpose: Beaconhouse sports competition information and event resources.

9) BISC RESULTS ARCHIVE
Official: https://www.beaconhouse.net/results/
Public categories include athletics, futsal, swimming, basketball, table tennis, quiz, idol, BISClick, Exploratorium, Biennale, BISC Film and BISC Talk.

10) LEARNER AGENCY PARADIGM (LAP)
About: https://lap.beaconhouse.net/about-us/
2026 guidelines: https://lap.beaconhouse.net/guidelines-2/
2027 guidelines: https://lap.beaconhouse.net/guidelines-ilap-2027/
Public description: Learner Agency Paradigm emphasizes student ownership, initiative, empathy, social responsibility, practical action and purposeful learning. Public LAP pages describe regional, national and international conference stages.

11) SCHOOL OF TOMORROW (SOT)
Official: https://www.beaconhouse.net/international-events-trips/
Public description: a free-to-public Beaconhouse event series organized since 2000 around education, culture, future-focused thinking and public dialogue.

12) EDUCATIONAL TRIPS
Official: https://www.beaconhouse.net/education-trips/
Public description: international activity programmes and learning tours combining educational, cultural and recreational experiences.

13) INTERNSHIP PROGRAMME
Official: https://www.beaconhouse.net/internship-programme/
Public description: Beaconhouse public internship resources for student development.

14) UNIVERSITY PLACEMENTS & SCHOLARSHIPS
Official: https://www.beaconhouse.net/university-placements-scholarships/
Public description: university guidance, placements and scholarship-related resources.

15) CLUBS & SOCIETIES
Official: https://www.beaconhouse.net/clubs-and-societies/
