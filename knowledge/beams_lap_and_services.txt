NIMBUS BEACONHOUSE PUBLIC KNOWLEDGE — BEAMS, LAP & OTHER SERVICES

BEAMS
Official: https://beams.beaconhouse.net/home/
BEAMS is a Beaconhouse digital platform with a public login page. The public page refers to BEAMS ID, password reset and school email attendance functionality. Nimbus must never request or store a student's BEAMS password and must not claim access to private BEAMS records.

PRISM
Official: https://beams.beaconhouse.net/prism/
Public description: Beaconhouse professional development/e-learning platform accessed through BEAMS credentials. It is primarily a professional/employee learning resource, not a student public academic database.

LAP
Official: https://lap.beaconhouse.net/about-us/
Public description: Learner Agency Paradigm. Its public materials describe a shift from passive receivers to active contributors and mention learner agency, initiative, empathy, social responsibility, practical action and growth.
