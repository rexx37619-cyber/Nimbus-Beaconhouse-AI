How to launch NimTron

Download the ZIP and extract it completely before launching the app.

1. Download NimTron-Desktop.
2. Extract the ZIP.
3. Open the extracted dist folder.
4. Open win-unpacked.
5. Double-click NimTron.exe.

Keep the entire win-unpacked folder together. Do not move only the EXE by itself.

System requirements

Windows 10/11 • approximately 200 MB installed size • microphone and camera optional • internet connection required for AI features.
